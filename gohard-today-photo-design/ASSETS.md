# Photo assets

Three original generated editorial photographs were created for this design handoff on 13 October 2026. They have no embedded UI or text. They are decorative illustrations, not real user activity or meal photographs. Do not attribute them to a stock photographer or claim a stock license.

The files retain their full 1536 × 1024 dimensions and were encoded as WebP for app packaging. The PNG generation outputs remain available separately; this package uses the optimized WebP copies. No crop or compositing was applied to the assets.

| Asset | Content | Crop guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| workout-hero.webp | Dumbbells and a bench in a quiet gym | Keep the lower center/right dumbbells visible. A wide crop may need a lower vertical focal point. Apply an appropriate text scrim. |
| run-cover.webp | Lower legs and unbranded shoes on an outdoor path | Preserve the foreground shoe around center/right; avoid cropping it into an unrecognizable fragment. |
| nutrition-cover.webp | A chicken, grain and vegetable bowl | Preserve the bowl around center/right. Use only as decorative nutrition imagery, including when no food is logged. |

Inspect the crop at the actual card dimensions in the app. The dark mockup is not a substitute for Light-mode, large-text or device checks.

## Generation briefs

- Workout: realistic black rubber hex dumbbells beside a padded gym bench; natural side light; subdued charcoal colors and textured floor.
- Running: realistic rear view of a runner's lower legs and shoes on a riverside/park path at morning light; anatomically correct two legs; no visible face.
- Nutrition: realistic grilled chicken, greens, tomatoes, grains and vegetables in a matte dark bowl; natural window light and a charcoal table.

All briefs requested standalone landscape photographs without text, logos, UI, borders or watermarks.
