# Leon v2 — still model for appearance review

Built 20 Nov 2026 after the owner rejected the v1 look (`design/leon-3d/`, kept unchanged for comparison).
Stills only: no animation and no app work until the owner approves the appearance. The squat movement still
needs a qualified technique review.

## Rebuild

```powershell
& C:\dev\tools\blender-4.5.16-windows-x64\blender.exe -b --factory-startup --python design\leon-3d\v2\build_leon_v2.py -- `
  --base C:\dev\tools\human-base-meshes\human-base-meshes-bundle-v1.4.1\human_base_meshes_bundle.blend `
  --stills C:\dev\GoHard-leon-prototype\v2\stills --res 1080 --out C:\dev\GoHard-leon-prototype\v2\leon_v2_still.blend
```

About a minute. The `.blend` (26 MB, mostly fur strands) is kept outside git at
`C:\dev\GoHard-leon-prototype\v2\leon_v2_still.blend`; the script rebuilds it exactly (fixed random seed).

## Source of the body

Blender Studio "Human Base Meshes" bundle v1.4.1, realistic male, **CC0** (public domain), downloaded from the
official mirror `download.blender.org/demo/asset-bundles/human-base-meshes/` (zip SHA-256
`811f43accbb31a88266d932f8f5563b2d13586fca0ba2693aad1f5fe582b3515`). Everything else is generated by the script.

## What the script does

- **Body:** sculpted multires level 2 of the base mesh; muscle forms strengthened by a band-pass of the surface pushed
  along the normal, plus added volume for delts, traps, lats, pecs, arms, quads, hamstrings and calves, a narrower
  waist and a thicker neck. A cavity field darkens muscle separations in the fur shader; chest and belly are paler.
- **Rig:** root, hips, spine, chest, neck, head, tail, clavicles, arms, hands, thighs, shins, feet, toes; bone-heat
  weights on the low-resolution base mesh, transferred to the subdivided body. No finger bones yet.
- **Head:** metaball forms (skull, brow, cheeks, muzzle, nose, lids, ears) with eyes, lash lines, brows and mouth;
  short fur strands on head, ears, brows and muzzle, cream chin fur, whiskers; a mane of about 70,000 strands
  (swept-back mane, face ruff, forehead crest) kept above the shoulders.
- **Clothing:** shorts and trainers are shells of the body surface (offset and smoothed), so they fit exactly and carry
  the body's skin weights. GoHard blue `#0062CC` stripe on the shorts and heel tab on the trainers; separate soles.
- **Body fur:** 170,000 short strands grown on the posed surface (static, for stills; animation will need
  surface-deformed curves).

## Known gaps (see the review page)

The head reads as a stylised mascot head on a realistic body; nose, inner ears and eyes look moulded; the mane is
straight strands rather than groomed locks; the body is leaner than the athletic reference; fingers are not rigged.
A head at the artwork's quality needs interactive sculpting and grooming by a 3D artist.
