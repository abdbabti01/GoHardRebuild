# Exercise Animatic coverage of GoHard's built-in exercises

Checked 21 November 2026 against Exercise Animatic's published exercise list (`UPDATED LIST.xlsx`, linked from
https://www.exerciseanimatic.com/free-fitness-exercise-videos, 2,970 rows, about 2,340 names once male and female
versions are merged) and each product page. Every link below opened on that day and showed the title quoted. Nothing
was bought and the supplier was not contacted.

- **Prices as listed on 21 Nov 2026**, during the site's "Black Friday month" promotion; they may change. Every single
  video checked showed **$1.00**. The Ultimate Bundle
  ([product page](https://www.exerciseanimatic.com/product-page/complete-2000-exercise-videos-lifetime-unlimited-license-workout-yoga-animation-exercise-fitness-gym))
  showed **$599.00** for the "2,600+" library.
- **What a single video page states**: "4K and a 2K resolution video file", MP4 H.264, muscles highlighted, the
  preview's watermark removed in the purchased file, and the licence "Non-Exclusive Commercial License (N-ECL) /
  Suitable for monetization" for "Mobile apps". Read the licence and the Terms of Service before buying; this table is
  not legal advice.
- **"Exact"** means the product title names the same equipment and the same movement as the catalogue key. Nobody has
  watched the paid previews, so each exact match still needs a look before it is bought. **No technique has been
  reviewed by a professional**, here or in the app.
- Most videos also exist in a female version (same title + "Female").
